Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Milwaukee Restoration Co provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Milwaukee, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Why Professional Sewage Cleanup Matters

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Do not wait on a slow drain, which can turn into a full blockage

How to Tell You Need Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Drain openings or basement slabs coated with visible sewage residue
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
